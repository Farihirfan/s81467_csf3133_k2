About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was renamed Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. On 1 May 2001, the institution was granted autonomy as a university college under the name KUSTEM, before being upgraded to a full-fledged public university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.

Located along the South China Sea coastline, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier university focusing on marine science, aquatic resources, maritime management, and environmental sustainability.
