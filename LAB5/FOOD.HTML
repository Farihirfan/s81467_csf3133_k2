About The Festival

The Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an annual celebration of traditional and modern Terengganu cuisine. Visitors can experience authentic local dishes, cultural performances and exciting activities throughout the event.

The festival gathers food entrepreneurs from across the state, providing visitors with an opportunity to taste famous dishes while supporting local businesses. Families, tourists and students are welcome to enjoy the vibrant atmosphere.

🎉 Free Entry For All Visitors!
